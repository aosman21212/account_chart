🏗️ Choose Your Business Model

Select the type of business from six pre-built templates. The wizard immediately shows a full preview of every account that will be created — before anything is changed.

  • Construction & Contracting — WIP, retentions, subcontractors
  • Real Estate & Rent — investment properties, tenant deposits
  • Trading & Retail — inventory, POS, trade payables
  • Manufacturing — raw materials, WIP, factory overhead
  • Professional Services — unbilled revenue, retainers
  • Restaurant & F&B — food cost, aggregator commissions

📋 Preview Before You Commit

Click Load Preview to see the full list of accounts before anything is created or deleted. The wizard tells you exactly:

⚠️ Replace mode
  • How many accounts will be permanently deleted (no entries)
  • How many accounts will be archived (have journal history)
  • How many new accounts will be created
  • How many account groups will be set up

Code conflicts are handled automatically — if an existing archived account already uses the same code, a unique suffix (A, B, …) is added and flagged in the preview.

🗂️ Automatic Account Groups

Account groups are created automatically alongside the accounts — giving you a clean two-level hierarchy for financial reports right from the start.

  • Level 1 — single-digit prefix (1, 2 … 6)
    Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Cost, Expenses
  • Level 2 — two-digit prefix (11, 12 … 64)
    Business-specific sub-categories that match the actual account code ranges
💡 Odoo auto-assigns parents
Group 11 automatically becomes a child of 1 — no manual linking needed.
